# Glossary

## Introduction
A quick reference guide for the common terms and jargon used in the X ecosystem.

## Core Concepts
- **Algorithm:** The set of rules used by X to determine content ranking.
- **Bookmarks:** A private way for users to save posts; a high-weight engagement signal.
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** The percentage of users who clicked on a link or profile after seeing a post.
- **Engagement Rate:** The total engagements divided by impressions.
- **For You Feed:** The algorithmically curated timeline.
- **Heavy Ranker:** The main machine learning model used for final ranking of posts.
- **Impressions:** The number of times a post appeared on a screen.
- **Out-of-Network:** Content from users that a specific user does not follow.
- **Ratio:** When a post has significantly more replies than likes, often indicating a negative reaction.
- **Shadowban:** An unofficial term for when a post or account's visibility is restricted without notice.
- **Thread:** A series of connected posts from the same user.

## Examples
- **High-Signal Engagement:** When a user bookmarks a thread instead of just liking it, signalling deep interest.
- **The "Ratio":** A post with 100 likes but 1,000 replies is likely being "ratioed" due to a controversial or unpopular take.

## Practical Takeaways
- Use this glossary to better understand analytics reports and platform updates.
- Understanding these terms helps you communicate more effectively with other creators.
