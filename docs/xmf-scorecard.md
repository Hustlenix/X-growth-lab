# X Momentum Scorecard (XMF-S)

## Introduction
The **X Momentum Scorecard** is a diagnostic tool used to measure the health of your content engine. By scoring each of the 5 pillars, you can identify exactly where your growth is leaking and where you need to focus your energy.

## The Assessment
For each pillar, score yourself from **0 to 20** based on the following criteria.

### 1. Signal Generation
- **[0-5]:** Most posts get views but almost no bookmarks or replies.
- **[6-10]:** Occasional bookmarks on "best" posts, but mostly likes.
- **[11-15]:** Consistent bookmarks and at least 2-3 replies per post.
- **[16-20]:** Posts regularly generate 10+ bookmarks and active discussions.
**Your Score:** \_\_\_\_/20

### 2. Algorithmic Velocity
- **[0-5]:** Posts get engagement slowly over several days.
- **[6-10]:** Most engagement happens in the first 4-6 hours.
- **[11-15]:** Clear "spike" of engagement in the first 60 minutes.
- **[16-20]:** Content often "takes off" immediately with rapid signal accumulation.
**Your Score:** \_\_\_\_/20

### 3. Out-of-Network Expansion
- **[0-5]:** Content is only seen by existing followers.
- **[6-10]:** Occasional "For You" feed appearances.
- **[11-15]:** 30-50% of impressions come from non-followers.
- **[16-20]:** Viral reach; most impressions are regularly from non-followers.
**Your Score:** \_\_\_\_/20

### 4. Profile Conversion
- **[0-5]:** High profile visits but almost no new followers.
- **[6-10]:** Conversion rate is around 1-2%.
- **[11-15]:** Conversion rate is healthy (3-5%).
- **[16-20]:** High-efficiency profile; >5% conversion from visit to follow.
**Your Score:** \_\_\_\_/20

### 5. Community Retention
- **[0-5]:** Followers rarely engage with new posts.
- **[6-10]:** A small group of "regulars" always engage.
- **[11-15]:** Strong "Inner Circle" that provides consistent initial distribution.
- **[16-20]:** Highly loyal community; every post starts with a massive signal boost from followers.
**Your Score:** \_\_\_\_/20

---

## Final Momentum Score
**Total Score:** \_\_\_\_/100

| Score | Tier | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0–20** | **Starting** | Foundation not yet built. Focus on Signal Generation. |
| **21–40** | **Emerging** | Identifying what works. Focus on consistency and hooks. |
| **41–60** | **Growing** | Momentum is building. Focus on Profile Conversion. |
| **61–80** | **Scaling** | Systems are working. Focus on Expansion and Velocity. |
| **81–100** | **Dominant** | Self-sustaining flywheel. Focus on Retention and high-level strategy. |
