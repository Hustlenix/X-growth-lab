# XMF Action Planner

## Introduction
The **XMF Action Planner** translates your [Scorecard](xmf-scorecard.md) results into a specific roadmap. Use this to focus your limited time on the pillar that will provide the highest return on investment.

---

## Targeted Recommendations

### 🔴 If your lowest score is: Signal Generation
**The Problem:** Your content isn't providing enough "Utility" or "Conversation" value.
**Actions:**
- Move from "Generic Advice" to "Specific Case Studies."
- Use the **Hook-Body-CTA** framework more strictly.
- Aim for 1 "Resource" post (List/Tutorial) per week to boost bookmarks.

### 🔴 If your lowest score is: Algorithmic Velocity
**The Problem:** Your content isn't accumulating engagement fast enough to trigger the expansion phase.
**Actions:**
- Schedule posts for when you are 100% available to reply to initial comments.
- Use a stronger, more provocative "Hook" to grab attention immediately.
- Cross-promote your new posts in your "Inner Circle" or niche communities.

### 🔴 If your lowest score is: Out-of-Network Expansion
**The Problem:** You are trapped in your follower bubble.
**Actions:**
- Reply to 10-20 larger accounts in your niche daily (High-quality replies).
- Write threads that address "Broad-but-Relevant" topics to attract the "For You" feed.
- Optimize your content for "Quote Retweets" by sharing strong opinions.

### 🔴 If your lowest score is: Profile Conversion
**The Problem:** You are getting reach, but people aren't sticking around.
**Actions:**
- **Audit your Bio:** Does it clearly state the value you provide?
- **Pinned Post:** Ensure your pinned post is your highest-value "Signal Generation" post.
- **Visuals:** Use a professional/clear profile picture and banner.

### 🔴 If your lowest score is: Community Retention
**The Problem:** Your followers are becoming "Ghost Followers."
**Actions:**
- Use the **Personality** pillar more: Share your story, failures, and wins.
- Host a Q&A or ask open-ended questions to your existing audience.
- Reply to *every* comment on your posts for at least 30 minutes after publishing.

## Weekly Momentum Worksheet
Use this simple structure to plan your week:

| Pillar | Focus Action | Success Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Signal** | Create one "How-To" Thread | 10+ Bookmarks |
| **Velocity** | Post at 9:00 AM (Peak Time) | 20 Replies in Hour 1 |
| **Expansion** | 50 High-Value Replies to others | 500+ Profile Visits |
| **Conversion** | Rewrite Bio | 5% Follow Rate |
| **Retention** | Share 1 "Build in Public" story | 20+ Returning Engagers |
