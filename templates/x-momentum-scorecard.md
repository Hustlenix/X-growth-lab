## 📈 Tiers & Actions

- **0–20 (Starting):** Focus on **Signal Generation**. Make content "bookmark-worthy."
- **21–40 (Emerging):** Focus on **Velocity**. Improve your hooks and timing.
- **41–60 (Growing):** Focus on **Profile Conversion**. Audit your bio and CTA.
- **61–80 (Scaling):** Focus on **Expansion**. Experiment with broader topics.
- **81–100 (Dominant):** Focus on **Retention**. Deepen community ties.
